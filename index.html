Our Love Story

10 years, forever to go

Cheldi and Jayson met in 2016, and what started as a simple connection grew into something truly special. Though shy at first, Jayson eventually confessed his feelings when Cheldi playfully asked. From that moment, their love began to grow.

Over the years, their bond grew stronger through their shared faith, motorcycle rides, travels, and the simple moments they cherished together.

After 10 years, Jayson proposed on October 31, 2025, during a trip at Villa Jovita Resort in Batangas. When Cheldi saw the shirt under his jacket that read “Will you marry me?”, she was completely surprised and without hesitation, she said Yes.

Now, they begin their forever.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why RSVP is so important?

To ensure we have the perfect seating and enough refreshments for everyone, please kindly confirm your attendance. Having an accurate guest list allows us to finalize our arrangements. Please RSVP by October 12, 2026.

Can I bring someone with me?

We do apologize but this is an invite-only event. As much as we would love to spend this day with all the people we love, we only have limited resources, so we opt to spend this day with the people who are most dear to us as a couple.

Can I bring my kids?

Although we love your children, we regretfully cannot accommodate them at the venue due to limited seating. We hope you are able to join us anyway and enjoy a relaxed evening with us.

Is there a dress code?

We kindly ask guests to dress in a semi-formal attire with a color of our motif. For more details, please refer to the Dress Code section on this website.

What time should I arrive?

The Processional will start at 2:45 PM and the ceremony will begin at 3:00 PM. Please arrive 15–30 minutes prior to Processional start time to assure you do not miss anything and allow us to begin on time.

When is the appropriate time to leave?

It would be much appreciated if you stay until the end of the program. We have planned to have this lovely wedding together with our selected guests. Please do not eat and run! *kidding*
